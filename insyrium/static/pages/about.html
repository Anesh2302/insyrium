Our story

Why we started

2019

The idea

A small team got tired of juggling passwords, chat apps and separate admin panels. One secure door into everything was the dream.

2021

First portal

Insyrium 1.0 shipped with email OTP, short-lived sessions and a minimal audit trail.

2023

Community & products

Built-in chat arrived, and the first sibling products — Decisium and MIRADS Builder — joined the family.

2026

Today

Teams worldwide sign in through Insyrium every day, backed by a brute-force watchdog and a complete audit trail.
